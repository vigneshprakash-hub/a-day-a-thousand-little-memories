A Letter You Can Keep

You may never truly understand how much you mean to me.

Thank you for being a part of my life, for the smiles you unknowingly gave me, and for the memories that still make my heart feel warm.

Some people come into our lives and leave without changing anything. But you... you changed something in me. You made ordinary days feel special.

I don't expect perfection from you. I don't expect you to always have the right words or always know what I'm feeling. I just hope you know that your existence has been a comfort in ways I can't explain.

No matter where life takes us, a part of my heart will always be grateful for you. If one day we become strangers, I'll still smile whenever I remember the chapter of my life that had you in it.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope your dreams come true. I hope you find the happiness you deserve, even if I'm not there to witness it.

And if you ever wonder whether you mattered to someone, the answer is yes.

You mattered to me.
