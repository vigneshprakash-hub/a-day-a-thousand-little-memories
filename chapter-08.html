All the Good Things Ahead

“ More Than I Prayed For “

I never knew how deeply I could love someone other than my blood relatives, my family.

But after I met you, I came to know how special it feels to share every moment with you, making each and every second feel full of love and joy.

I always manifested someone who would make me feel at home, somewhere other than home, Like I wanted to experience that feeling. I got more than I prayed for.

That is your kind of love, in so many forms — like a friend, a sister, a supporter, like a second mother, like a soul friend - my second soul for whom I can't even find the words to explain this feeling that I'm getting from you.

“I hope your life keeps bringing you wonderful people to love, laugh with, and grow beside, because someone else becoming important to you will never make you less important to me.”
